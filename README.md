# The Funniest Sick-Day Excuse

One line under 200 characters that gets you out of work and gets a laugh. The entry is a small Python program that writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, where the competition's sealed scorer reads it.

Competition: https://make.fun/api/competitions/u-zeroxfive-the-funniest-sick-day-excuse-37423a36/requirements

## Entry

> Sorry, I can't come in today — my smart fridge locked me out of the kitchen and is holding my lunch hostage until I update its firmware.
